source-bound informational review role is an active product decision. This
audit already supplies its minimum contract proposal below; no separate drafting
task is needed. Keep any informational role separate from the Sol
release-authority path. Its minimum requirements are:

   served model/provider/route, request/response hashes, status/finish/usage,

   Any bounded model rendition needs an explicit transform/hash/dimension

   known uncertainty/schema limitations, without turning flags into recrop,
   deletion or approval authority. No human-review reduction is yet measured.

   approve/recrop/unresolved actions. Preserve every-candidate review, manual
   veto, receipt integrity and separate source-deletion authority. A valid
   semantic fail may be source-overridden; unavailable receipts remain held.
