## Approach and research

Exact source identity binding and explicit lexical/document qualifiers belong in
code. Typed decision models could later help uncertain scope classification but
introduce provider/contract/unknown-outcome and calibration requirements; language
models add cost and rewrite risk without helping these explicit identity defects.
No AI failure claim. Primary methods reused: Python re lexical boundaries
https://docs.python.org/3/library/re.html , WHATWG document fragment target
identity https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/browsing-the-web.html#scroll-to-the-fragment-identifier ,
and Sphinx explicit/document-scoped crossrefs
https://www.sphinx-doc.org/en/master/usage/referencing.html .

Identifiers remain complete/case-folded, never stripped numeric guesses.
Scope grammar requires explicit foreign modifiers and attached document nouns;
clear local clauses stay eligible. Unknown titles/pronouns/general sentence
interpretation are outside this grammar. Synthetic prefixed labels vary beyond
r/e (Q42/APP7/Appendix12.3a); duplicate, dotted, wrong-but-valid, alias authority,
inline markup, index, range and prose-negative controls exercise the class.
